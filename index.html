TaskBell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Summary

  • TaskBell is a to-do and reminder app. We keep only what the app needs to work.
  • We do not show ads, sell your data, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your attachments, background pictures and reminders stay on your phone.
  • If you sign in, your tasks are saved in our cloud database so they sync between your devices.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time. See Delete your account.

This policy is from AppZone ("we", "us"), the developer of TaskBell. Contact: appzone365@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy
Email address If you create an account with email, or sign in with Google To sign you in
Name If you enter one, or it comes from your Google account Shown to people in the shared groups you join
User ID (like TB-7K4M-9QXP) Created automatically for signed-in accounts So other people can invite you to a shared group

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, device advertising ID or payment details.

What stays on your phone

These are never uploaded to our servers:

  • Attachments you add to a task.
  • Your own background photo chosen on the Theme screen.
  • Reminders: they are scheduled by your phone, not sent from a server.
  • Home-screen widget data.
  • Everything, if the app is used without an account on a build with no cloud connection.

Voice input

When you tap the microphone, TaskBell uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Google's on Android, Apple's on iOS) to turn what you say into the task title. Your audio may be processed by that provider under its own privacy policy. TaskBell receives only the resulting text and does not record or store audio.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to remind you about tasks.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the time you set.
  • Microphone: only when you use voice input.
  • Photos: only when you choose your own background picture.

Each permission is optional. The rest of the app works without it.

Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account and task data for us. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Other TaskBell users, but only within a shared group you have joined: they see your name, your User ID, the group's tasks and its history. Nobody can search for you by name or email; they need your exact User ID.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database security rules allow each person to read only their own data and the groups they belong to.

Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open TaskBell and open the side menu (tap your initials on the Home screen).
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or by choosing your Google account again, then tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Email appzone365@gmail.com with the subject "TaskBell account deletion", from the email address you sign in with, or include your User ID. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • All your tasks, notes and categories.
  • Your User ID and name.
  • Groups you own, for every member, with their tasks and history.
  • Invitations you sent or received.
  • Everything TaskBell kept on that phone.

What is kept

When you leave a group someone else owns, the shared tasks you added to it, and the group's history entries that show your name, stay with that group for its other members. They are removed when the group's owner deletes the group.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete it, the data listed above is deleted straight away from our database. Copies in Google's backups can take a while longer to disappear.

Children

TaskBell is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can view and change your tasks in the app at any time, and delete your account as described above. To ask for a copy of your data, or for anything else about your privacy, email appzone365@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also let you know in the app.
